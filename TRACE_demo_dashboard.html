Hyperspectral Imaging: Checkpoint Inspection

This is the actual imaging layer. A hyperspectral camera captures a pixel grid over the pallet or product surface at each physical hand-off point. Every pixel carries its own 8-band spectral signature, which gets reduced to a per-pixel Quality Index using an NDVI-style band ratio. A simple threshold rule (basic computer vision, no machine learning) flags degraded pixels and reports the defect area as a percentage.
